        - How many rows? → Outer loop.
        - What should each row contain? → Inner loop(s).

2. One thing to learn For symmetric patterns, think in sections instead of loops.
For every row:
[spaces] [left] [right]


Then decide the count for each section:
Spaces = n - i - 1
Left stars = i + 1
Right stars = i

Row 1 (i = 1) -   ***
[spaces][left][right]
    3      2      1

Row 2 (i = 2) -  *****
[spaces][left][right]
    2      3      2

Row 3 (i = 3) - *******
[spaces][left][right]
    1      4      3

Row 4 (i = 4) -*********
[spaces][left][right]
    0      5      4


The important lesson is don't think "I need 3 loops." Think:

Each row has three parts: spaces, left stars, right stars. How many characters does each part have?


This way, even more complex patterns (diamonds, hourglasses, butterflies) become just combinations of sections rather than something to memorize.
